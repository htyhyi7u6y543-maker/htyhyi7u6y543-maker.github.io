Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clips Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that uploads short video clips to its owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What data the app uses

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Google after the owner signs in. They are used only to upload videos to the owner's own accounts.
  • Basic account information (TikTok display name, YouTube channel name) to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video files and captions that the owner prepared on their own computer.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the owner's computer. The app has no server and does not send data anywhere except to TikTok and Google, through their official APIs, to upload the owner's videos.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use data for advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and in the Google account settings (Security → Third-party apps). Deleting the app's local files removes all stored tokens.
